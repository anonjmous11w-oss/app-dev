**Harry Potter** is a fantasy series written by **J.K. Rowling**, following a young wizard who discovers his magical heritage and attends Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry.
The story explores friendship, courage, and the battle between good and evil through seven books and eight films.

 ### Main Characters 
**Harry Potter** – The Boy Who Lived, known for his lightning‑shaped scar.

**Hermione Granger** – Intelligent and resourceful, a loyal friend.

**Ron Weasley** – Brave and humorous, Harry’s best friend.

### 🪄 Themes
Magic, friendship, bravery, and the importance of choice.

**Philosopher’s Stone (1997)** – Harry discovers he’s a wizard and begins his journey at Hogwarts.

**Chamber of Secrets (1998)** – A hidden chamber threatens students at Hogwarts.

**Prisoner of Azkaban (1999)** – Harry learns about Sirius Black and his family’s past.

**Goblet of Fire (2000)** – Harry competes in the dangerous Triwizard Tournament.

**Order of the Phoenix (2003)** – Harry forms Dumbledore’s Army to resist Voldemort.

**Half‑Blood Prince (2005)** – Secrets of Voldemort’s past and Horcruxes are revealed.

**Deathly Hallows (2007)** – The final battle between Harry and Voldemort.
